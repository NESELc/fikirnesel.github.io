## Üretim istemleri

### brain
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: wide editorial website card cover, landscape 3:2. Dark navy blue printed circuit board viewed straight from above. Fine copper circuit traces form the distinct silhouette of a human brain with two hemispheres and intricate branching folds, integrated naturally into the PCB. A few restrained blue LEDs. Elegant realistic macro materials, sharp recognizable brain shape, uncluttered composition, subject centered with generous margins for a wide crop. No lettering, no text, no logos, no watermark.

### food
Use case: product-mockup. Website card photo, wide 3:2 composition. A small tasteful arrangement of fresh apple, pear, carrot, broccoli and two walnuts on a clean pale warm white background. Natural food photography, soft daylight, realistic textures, uncluttered, all food fully visible in central area with generous negative space. No plate, no lettering, no logos, no watermark.

### meter
Use case: product-mockup. Wide editorial website thumbnail. Realistic modern single-phase DIN-rail electricity energy meter, compact rectangular light gray enclosure, small subdued green-gray LCD reading 01234 with kWh unit, one small blue button, four recessed screw terminals in a protected terminal strip, clearly identifiable professional electrical measurement device. Straight-on product photography, precise industrial design, soft neutral white background, gentle shadow, lots of breathing room. No appliance cord, no loose plug, no decorative coil, no invented brand or logo, no watermark. Entire device visible, natural proportions, not a cartoon, not a toy, not a rounded smart speaker.

### battery
Use case: scientific-educational. Edit the Baghdad Battery illustrative reconstruction into a clean cutaway view. Keep the warm ivory background, terracotta and copper material style, soft museum lighting. Replace separate disassembled components with ONE assembled terracotta jar shown in three-quarter cutaway: front quarter of ceramic wall removed so its thickness is visible, an upright hollow copper cylinder INSIDE the jar, copper cylinder also cut away in front to reveal a single central dark iron rod, dark bitumen stopper at the narrow neck around the rod. Rod and copper separated by a clearly visible gap. Closed copper base resting inside the jar. Entire jar visible with breathing room. No liquid, no wires, no sparks, no modern battery, no labels, no text, no watermark. Historically inspired conceptual reconstruction, not archival evidence. Landscape 3:2 framing.
